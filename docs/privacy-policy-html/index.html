Privacy Policy for Vibration Meter

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vibration Meter. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Vibration Meter is developed and maintained by Cihat Akyol. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address provided at the end of this document.

2. Information We Collect

Sensor Data

Vibration Meter collects and processes the following data:

  • Accelerometer Readings: The app accesses your device's accelerometer sensor to measure vibration levels in meters per second squared (m/s²). This data includes three-axis acceleration measurements (X, Y, and Z axes) and computed magnitude values.
  • Measurement Sessions: When you record a measurement session, the app stores:
    • Session name (user-provided)
    • Start and end timestamps
    • Accelerometer readings captured during the session
    • Statistical data (peak values, averages, duration)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Contact information
  • Photos, media, or other files
  • Network or Wi-Fi information beyond what's required for basic app functionality

3. How We Use Your Information

The accelerometer data collected by Vibration Meter is used exclusively for:

  • App Functionality: Displaying real-time vibration measurements on your device screen
  • Session Recording: Storing measurement sessions that you choose to save
  • Data Visualization: Generating graphs and statistics from recorded sessions
  • Data Export: Enabling you to export your session data for your own use

We do not use your data for:

  • Marketing or advertising
  • Analytics or user tracking
  • Profiling or behavioral analysis
  • Sharing with third parties
  • Any purpose other than the core app functionality

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only

All data collected by Vibration Meter is stored locally on your device only. The app uses:

  • Room Database: A secure local database to store measurement sessions
  • DataStore: Local preference storage for app settings

Important: No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties. Your measurement data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export and share it.

Data Security

  • Device Encryption: Your data benefits from your device's built-in encryption capabilities
  • No Network Transmission: Since no data is sent over the network, there is no risk of interception during transmission
  • Access Control: Only you have access to your measurement data through the app interface

5. User Rights and Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

Data Access

You can access all your stored measurement sessions through the app's session history screen at any time.

Data Export

You can export your session data using the app's built-in export functionality. Exported data can be shared via your device's standard sharing mechanisms (email, cloud storage, etc.).

Data Deletion

You can delete any measurement session at any time through the app interface:

  • Individual sessions can be deleted from the session detail screen
  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the application or clearing app data in your device settings

Data Portability

All exported data is in standard formats that can be used with other applications or stored for your records.

6. Third-Party Services

Vibration Meter does not integrate any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Cloud storage services
  • Social media integrations

The app operates completely independently and does not communicate with external services.

7. Children's Privacy

Vibration Meter does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is rated for Everyone and does not contain content targeted at children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app if needed.

Because no personal information or identifiable data is collected, there are no special considerations for children's data under COPPA or similar regulations.

8. International Users and Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers, there are no international data transfers. Your data remains within your device's storage regardless of your geographic location.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

13. Additional Information

Data Retention

Measurement sessions are retained on your device indefinitely until you delete them manually or uninstall the app. There is no automatic deletion or expiration of data.

Permissions

The app requires access to your device's accelerometer sensor. This is a standard hardware feature and does not require special permission on most devices. No other sensitive permissions are requested.

Summary: Vibration Meter is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect only the sensor data necessary for the app to function, store everything locally on your device, and never share your data with anyone. You have complete control over your data at all times.
