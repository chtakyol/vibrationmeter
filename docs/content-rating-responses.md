## Common Questions

**Q: What if I add a privacy policy link in the app later?**
A: You should update the questionnaire to indicate "Yes, contains links" and specify it's a privacy policy link only. This typically doesn't change the rating.

**Q: Does the "Everyone" rating help or hurt downloads?**
A: Everyone rating is positive - it means the app is appropriate for all ages, giving you the widest possible audience.

**Q: How long does rating approval take?**
A: Usually instant via IARC system. If there are issues, it may take 1-2 business days.

**Q: Can I have different ratings in different regions?**
A: No, you answer one questionnaire and receive ratings for all regions automatically based on those answers.

**Q: What if I disagree with a rating?**
A: Ratings are automatic based on your responses. If you believe an answer was misunderstood, you can retake the questionnaire and clarify your answers.

## Renewal

Content ratings do not expire unless:
1. You change app content significantly
2. You add new features that affect ratings (ads, IAP, user interaction)
3. Rating authorities request re-certification

**Best Practice:** Review and update the questionnaire whenever you release major updates that might affect content.
